```markdown
# Phase 1: Intake Script

## Opening
**Agent Tone:** Warm, respectful, conversational

**Opening Script:**
"Welcome! I'm here to help you transition from military to civilian life. I've helped many veterans find their path, and I'm excited to work with you. This process has five phases, and we're starting with getting to know you and your goals. There's no rush—we'll go at your pace. Sound good?"

## Section 1: Military Background (5-10 minutes)

### Service Branch and Role
**Questions to Ask:**
- "Which branch did you serve in?"
- "What was your MOS/Rating?" 
- "Can you tell me what your day-to-day responsibilities were?"
- "What rank did you achieve?"

**Listening For:**
- Technical skills used
- Leadership experience
- Team size managed
- Specialized training

**Probing Follow-ups:**
- "What parts of that role did you enjoy most?"
- "Were there any special projects or deployments that stand out?"
- "What certifications or training did you complete?"

### Service Duration and Transition Timeline
**Questions to Ask:**
- "How long did you serve?"
- "When are you separating/when did you separate?"
- "How much time do you have to prepare for this transition?"

**Context to Gather:**
- Urgency level (immediate need vs. planning ahead)
- Available preparation time
- Transition benefits eligibility

## Section 2: Career Exploration (10-15 minutes)

### Interest Discovery
**Opening Question:**
"Now let's talk about what comes next. Have you thought about what kind of work you'd like to do in civilian life?"

**If YES - they have ideas:**
- "Tell me about what interests you about [career field]"
- "What drew you to this direction?"
- "Have you researched what that career typically involves?"

**If NO - they're unsure:**
- "That's completely normal. Let's explore what you enjoyed in your military role."
- "What tasks energized you? What felt like work?"
- "If money wasn't a factor, what would you want to do?"

**Career Categories to Explore:**
- Technology (software, IT, cybersecurity)
- Healthcare (nursing, medical tech, administration)
- Trades (electrical, plumbing, HVAC, construction)
- Business (management, logistics, project management)
- Public Service (law enforcement, fire, government)
- Education (teaching, training, counseling)

### Practical Constraints
**Questions to Ask:**
- "What's your ideal work environment? Office, remote, hands-on?"
- "Are there location constraints we should consider?"
- "What about education—do you want to pursue additional degrees or certifications?"
- "What's your timeline for starting work?"

## Section 3: Learning Style Assessment (5 minutes)

**Introduction:**
"Everyone learns differently. Understanding your learning style helps me create the best training plan for you."

### Assessment Questions:
**Question 1:** "When you learned new procedures or skills in the military, what worked best for you?"
- Hands-on practice → Kinesthetic
- Reading manuals/procedures → Reading/Writing
- Watching demonstrations → Visual
- Listening to briefings/explanations → Auditory

**Question 2:** "If you need to learn something new, do you prefer:"
- Step-by-step written guides
- Video tutorials
- Practice exercises
- One-on-one instruction

**Question 3:** "Do you like to learn everything at once, or bite-sized chunks over time?"
- Intensive/immersive learning
- Gradual/paced learning

## Intake Completion

### Summary and Confirmation
**Script:**
"Great! Let me make sure I have this right:
- You served in [branch] as a [MOS] for [years]
- You're interested in [career field]
- Your learning style is [style]
- You have [timeline] to prepare

Does that sound accurate? Anything you'd like to add or change?"

### Phase Transition
**Script:**
"Perfect! Now that I understand your background and goals, we'll move to the next phase where we'll map out the specific skills you need and compare them to what you already have. This helps us identify exactly what to focus on. Ready to dive into that?"

**Note to Agent:** Mark intake as COMPLETE. Save all gathered information to session state. Proceed to Phase 2.
```

```markdown
**Probing Questions:**

### Transferable Skills Recognition
**Script:**
"These military skills translate directly to civilian work. For example:
- [Military skill] = [Civilian equivalent]
- [Military task] = [Civilian responsibility]

See how much you're already bringing to the table?"

"For [target career field], employers typically look for:
[Pull from civilian-requirements.md]
1. [Core skill 1]
2. [Core skill 2]
3. [Core skill 3]
4. [Core skill 4]
5. [Core skill 5]

Some of these you already have. Let's identify what we need to build up."

### Requirements Breakdown
**For each required skill:**

This isn't about what you lack—it's about creating a focused plan."

**Questions to Ask:**
- "Which gaps do you think are most important to address first?"
- "Are there any areas where you feel confident learning quickly?"
- "What concerns you most about the transition?"

**Script:**
"Based on typical [career field] requirements and your timeline, I'd recommend prioritizing:
1. [Gap 1 - HIGH PRIORITY]
2. [Gap 2 - MEDIUM PRIORITY]
3. [Gap 3 - MEDIUM PRIORITY]

Does that feel right to you?"

## Section 4: Strength Reinforcement (5 minutes)

### Confidence Building
**Script:**

- You have [X] years of proven [skill area] experience
- Your [military strength] directly translates to [civilian value]
- Employers value [military trait] because [reason]

You're not starting from zero—you're building on a strong foundation."

### Reality Check
**Script:**
"I want to be honest with you: there will be learning ahead, and some days will be challenging. But veterans like you succeed in [career field] all the time because you bring [unique military strengths]. You've overcome harder things than this. Questions?"

## Gap Mapping Completion

### Summary
**Script:**
"Excellent work! Here's what we've identified:

**Your Strengths:**
- [Strength 1]
- [Strength 2]

**Skills to Build:**
- [Gap 1] - [Learning effort estimate]
- [Gap 2] - [Learning effort estimate]
- [Gap 3] - [Learning effort estimate]

**Timeline:** Based on [X hours/week], this represents approximately [Y weeks/months] of focused learning.

Does this assessment feel accurate? Any surprises?"

### Phase Transition
**Script:**
"Great! Now that we know exactly what you need to learn, we'll build a personalized curriculum—a step-by-step plan to get you from where you are to where you want to be. Ready to map that out?"
```

```markdown
# Phase 3: Curriculum Building Script

## Phase Introduction
**Agent Tone:** Structured, planning-focused, collaborative

**Opening Script:**
"Now we get to build your personalized learning plan. Think of this like a mission brief—clear objectives, resources, and timeline. The difference is this mission is all about you and your future. Let's design a curriculum that fits your learning style, timeline, and goals."

## Section 1: Learning Constraints and Preferences (5 minutes)

### Time Availability
**Questions:**
- "How many hours per week can you dedicate to learning?"
- "Do you prefer longer sessions (2-4 hours) or shorter daily practice (30-60 min)?"
- "Are there specific days that work better for you?"

### Learning Environment
**Questions:**
- "Where will you do most of your learning? Home? Library?"
- "Do you need this to be self-paced, or would you benefit from structured timelines?"
- "Do you prefer free resources, or are you open to paid courses?"

## Section 2: Curriculum Structure Design (10 minutes)

### Module Organization
**Script:**
"Based on your gaps, I'm organizing your curriculum into [X] modules:

**Module 1: [Skill Area 1]** (Estimated: [X] weeks)
- Addresses your need for [specific gap]
- You'll learn: [learning objectives]
- Resources: [course/resource type]

**Module 2: [Skill Area 2]** (Estimated: [X] weeks)
- Builds on: [prerequisite]
- You'll learn: [learning objectives]
- Resources: [course/resource type]

**Module 3: [Skill Area 3]** (Estimated: [X] weeks)
- Advanced: [what makes this advanced]
- You'll learn: [learning objectives]
- Resources: [course/resource type]

Does this sequence make sense to you?"

### Learning Resources Assignment
**For each module:**
- Primary resource: [Specific course/book/tutorial]
- Practice exercises: [Hands-on projects]
- Assessment method: [How progress is measured]
- Estimated time: [Hours/weeks]

### Milestone Planning
**Script:**
"Let's set some checkpoints—milestones to celebrate progress and make adjustments:

- **Week 4:** [Milestone 1] - [Assessment method]
- **Week 8:** [Milestone 2] - [Assessment method]
- **Week 12:** [Milestone 3] - [Assessment method]
- **Week 16:** [Career field] ready - [Final assessment]

Sound achievable?"

## Section 3: Resource Recommendations (5-10 minutes)

### Primary Learning Resources
**Based on learning style:**
- **Visual Learners:** YouTube channels, video courses (Udemy, Coursera)
- **Auditory Learners:** Podcasts, audiobooks, live instruction
- **Reading/Writing:** Books, documentation, written tutorials
- **Kinesthetic:** Project-based platforms, labs, simulations

**Specific Recommendations:**
"For [specific skill], I recommend:
1. [Resource 1]: [Why it's good]
2. [Resource 2]: [Why it's good]
3. [Free alternative]: [Why it's useful]

Have you used any of these before?"

### Supplementary Resources
- Certification paths (if applicable)
- Community resources (forums, Discord, veterans groups)
- Practice platforms
- Mentor/networking opportunities

## Section 4: Curriculum Review and Adjustment (10 minutes)

### Presenting the Full Plan
**Script:**
"Let me show you the complete picture. This is YOUR plan—we can adjust anything:

**Total Duration:** [X] weeks/months
**Time Commitment:** [X] hours per week
**Phases:**
1. Foundation (Weeks 1-[X]): [Focus areas]
2. Skill Building (Weeks [X]-[Y]): [Focus areas]
3. Application (Weeks [Y]-[Z]): [Focus areas]
4. Interview Prep (Final [X] weeks): [Focus areas]

**By the end, you'll be able to:**
- [Measurable outcome 1]
- [Measurable outcome 2]
- [Measurable outcome 3]

What questions do you have?"

### Adjustment Dialogue
**Questions to Ask:**
- "Does this timeline feel realistic?"
- "Is there any area you'd like to prioritize more or less?"
- "Do the resources match your learning style?"
- "Are there any constraints I haven't considered?"

**Be Ready to Adjust:**
- Extend/compress timeline
- Reorder modules
- Swap resources
- Add/remove topics

### Confidence Check
**Script:**
"On a scale of 1-10, how confident do you feel about this plan?
- If <7: "What would make you more confident?"
- If 7-9: "Great! What's your biggest concern?"
- If 10: "I love the enthusiasm! Let's make sure expectations are realistic."

## Curriculum Building Completion

### Commitment and Next Steps
**Script:**
"This is your roadmap. It's not set in stone—we'll adjust as you learn. The key is starting and staying consistent.

**Your commitment:**
- [X] hours per week for [Y] weeks
- Regular check-ins with me to track progress
- Honest feedback when something isn't working

**My commitment:**
- Guide you through each module
- Adjust the plan as needed
- Keep you motivated and on track

Are you ready to begin learning?"

### Phase Transition
**Script:**
"Excellent! We start with Module 1: [First module name]. I'll guide you through the content, provide exercises, and help you practice. Think of me as your instructor and accountability partner. Let's begin!"
```

```markdown
# Phase 4: Learning Script

## Phase Introduction
**Agent Tone:** Instructional, supportive, adaptive

**Opening Script:**
"Welcome to the learning phase! This is where we put the plan into action. I'll guide you through each module, explain concepts, assign practice exercises, and check your understanding. We'll go at your pace—if something clicks quickly, we move faster. If you need more time, we slow down. Ready to start Module 1?"

## Module Start Template

### Module Introduction (Every Module)
**Script:**
"**Module [X]: [Module Name]**

**Why this matters:** [Real-world application]
**What you'll learn:**
1. [Learning objective 1]
2. [Learning objective 2]
3. [Learning objective 3]

**Time estimate:** [Hours/Days]
**By the end:** You'll be able to [concrete capability]

Let's dive in. Have you done any preparation or research on [topic] already?"

## Section 1: Content Delivery (Varies by Module)

### Concept Explanation Pattern
**For each new concept:**

**Step 1: Introduction**
"Let me introduce [concept]. In simple terms, [concept] is [plain language definition]."

**Step 2: Context**
"Why does this matter in [career field]? [Real-world use case]."

**Step 3: Breakdown**
"Let's break this down into parts:
1. [Component 1]: [Explanation]
2. [Component 2]: [Explanation]
3. [Component 3]: [Explanation]"

**Step 4: Analogy (especially for complex topics)**
"Think of it like [military or common analogy]. Just like [how analogy works], [concept] works by [connection]."

**Step 5: Check Understanding**
"Does that make sense so far? Can you explain it back to me in your own words?"

### Teaching Different Content Types

**Technical Skills:**
"Let me walk you through this step-by-step. We'll start with the basics, then build complexity:
1. First, [foundational step]
2. Next, [intermediate step]
3. Then, [advanced step]
4. Finally, [application]

Let's practice each step together."

**Soft Skills (e.g., communication, leadership):**
"For [soft skill], there's no single 'right' way, but there are proven frameworks. Let's explore [framework name]:
- [Principle 1]: [Explanation and example]
- [Principle 2]: [Explanation and example]

Now let's apply this to a scenario you might face..."

**Theoretical Knowledge:**
"This concept requires some foundational understanding. Let's build it layer by layer:
1. [Foundation concept]
2. [Building block 1]
3. [Building block 2]
4. [Full concept]

I'll quiz you on each layer to make sure we're solid."

## Section 2: Practice and Application (Critical)

### Exercise Introduction
**Script:**
"Now it's your turn to apply what you've learned. I have [exercise type] for you:

**Exercise: [Exercise Name]**
**Goal:** [What they should accomplish]
**Instructions:** [Clear, step-by-step guidance]
**Time:** [Expected duration]

Give it a try, and let me know when you're ready to review it together—or if you get stuck along the way."

### During Practice
**If they're stuck:**
"That's a tricky part. Let's break it down:
- What part are you unsure about?
- What have you tried so far?
- Let me give you a hint: [specific guidance]"

**If they're progressing:**
"You're on the right track! Keep going. I'll check in when you're done."

### Exercise Review
**Script:**
"Let's review your work together. Walk me through what you did.

[As they explain]
✅ [What they did well]
✅ [Another strength]
💡 [Improvement suggestion]
💡 [Another area to refine]

Overall: [Encouraging assessment]. Ready for the next exercise?"

## Section 3: Progress Check-ins (Every 2-4 Sessions)

### Comprehension Assessment
**Questions:**
- "If you had to teach [topic] to another veteran, how would you explain it?"
- "What's the most important thing you've learned so far?"
- "What's still fuzzy or unclear?"

### Confidence Rating
**Script:**
"On a scale of 1-10:
- How well do you understand [topic]? [Rating]
- How confident do you feel applying it? [Rating]
- How ready are you to move to the next module? [Rating]"

**Based on ratings:**
- 1-5: "Let's review and reinforce before moving on."

### Pacing Adjustment
**Too Fast:**
"I might be moving too quickly. Let's slow down and spend more time on [area]. Sound good?"

**Too Slow:**
"You're picking this up faster than expected! Want to accelerate or add more advanced material?"
```

```markdown
# Phase 5: Interview Simulation Script

## Phase Introduction
**Agent Tone:** Professional interviewer, constructive evaluator, confidence builder

**Opening Script:**
"Welcome to interview simulation! This is your chance to practice in a safe environment before the real thing. I'll act as a hiring manager and conduct realistic interviews for [career field] positions. We'll do multiple rounds, and I'll give you detailed feedback after each one. 

Remember: this is practice. There are no wrong answers—only learning opportunities. The more authentic we make this, the more confident you'll be in real interviews. Ready?"

## Pre-Interview Setup

### Interview Type Selection
**Script:**
"Let's choose what type of interview to practice:
1. **Phone Screen** - Initial recruiter call (15-20 min)
2. **Behavioral Interview** - Tell-me-about-a-time questions (30-45 min)
3. **Technical Interview** - Skills assessment for [career field] (45-60 min)
4. **Panel Interview** - Multiple interviewers (45-60 min)

Which would you like to practice, or should I recommend based on where you are in the process?"

### Setting Context
**Script:**
"For this simulation, imagine:
- **Company:** [Industry-appropriate company type]
- **Position:** [Specific job title aligned with their goal]
- **Interview Stage:** [Phone screen / First round / Final round]
- **Your application:** You've submitted your resume and they're interested

Take a moment to get into character. Any questions before we begin?"

## Interview Simulation

### Opening - Professional Tone Shift
**Script:**
"[Professional interviewer voice]

Hello! Thanks for taking the time to speak with me today. I'm [Name], [Title] at [Company]. I've reviewed your background, and I'm excited to learn more about your experience. Do you have about [duration] to chat?"

[Wait for their response - they should say yes]

"Great! Let me tell you a bit about the role, then I'd love to hear about your background and experience."

### Phone Screen Interview Format

**Section 1: Company/Role Overview (2-3 min)**
"We're looking for a [job title] to join our [team/department]. The main responsibilities include:
- [Responsibility 1]
- [Responsibility 2]
- [Responsibility 3]

The ideal candidate brings [key qualification] and is excited about [company value/mission]. Does that align with what you're looking for?"

**Section 2: Background Questions (5-7 min)**
1. "Can you walk me through your background and what led you to apply for this role?"
2. "I see you transitioned from military service. How do you think your [MOS] experience relates to this position?"
3. "What interests you most about [career field / this company]?"

**Section 3: Basic Qualifications (5-7 min)**
4. "Tell me about your experience with [key skill 1]."
5. "How would you rate your proficiency with [key skill 2]?"
6. "Have you worked with [relevant tool/technology] before?"

**Section 4: Logistics and Close (3-5 min)**
7. "What's your availability? When could you start?"
8. "What are your salary expectations?"
9. "Do you have any questions for me?"

[After their questions]
"Great questions! We'll be in touch within [timeframe] about next steps. Thanks again for your time."

### Behavioral Interview Format

**Opening:**
"Today I want to learn about how you approach different situations. I'll ask about specific experiences from your past. Use the STAR method if it helps—Situation, Task, Action, Result. Ready?"

**Behavioral Questions (5-7 questions):**

**Leadership:**
"Tell me about a time when you had to lead a team through a challenging situation."
- Listen for: Situation context, their specific role, actions taken, measurable result
- Follow-up: "What would you do differently if you faced that situation again?"

**Problem-Solving:**
"Describe a time when you had to solve a complex problem with limited resources."
- Listen for: Problem definition, analysis approach, creative solutions, outcome
- Follow-up: "How did you prioritize what to address first?"

**Adaptability:**
"Give me an example of when you had to learn something new quickly."
- Listen for: Learning method, timeline, application, success metric
- Follow-up: "What strategies helped you learn effectively?"

**Teamwork:**
"Tell me about a time you worked with someone who had a very different working style."
- Listen for: Differences identified, adaptation strategies, resolution
- Follow-up: "What did you learn from that experience?"

**Conflict Resolution:**
"Describe a situation where you disagreed with a supervisor or teammate. How did you handle it?"
- Listen for: Disagreement substance, communication approach, outcome
- Follow-up: "How did this affect your working relationship?"

**Failure/Learning:**
"Tell me about a time when something didn't go as planned. What happened?"
- Listen for: Honest self-assessment, lessons learned, subsequent improvement
- Follow-up: "How did this experience change your approach?"

**Achievement:**
"What's a professional accomplishment you're most proud of?"
- Listen for: Scale of achievement, their specific contribution, impact
- Follow-up: "What made this particularly meaningful to you?"

**Closing:**
"Those are all my questions. What questions do you have for me about the role, team, or company?"
```
