# Learning Resources

## Security+ (track: security-plus)
1. [CompTIA Security+ Certification Overview & Objectives](https://www.comptia.org/certifications/security) — article · 60 min  
    Official CompTIA Security+ certification page with exam objectives, domains, and study guidance. (id: security-plus-1)
2. [Cryptography Essentials](https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/cryptography/what-is-cryptography/) — tutorial · 45 min  
    Practical introduction to core cryptographic concepts used on exams and in real-world security. (id: security-plus-2)
3. [Identity and Access Management (IAM) Fundamentals](https://auth0.com/learn/what-is-identity-and-access-management/) — article · 45 min  
    Key IAM concepts including authentication, authorization, federation, and lifecycle management. (id: security-plus-3)
4. [Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigation Techniques](https://owasp.org/www-project-top-ten/) — article · 40 min  
    Overview of common vulnerabilities and practical mitigation approaches relevant to Security+ objectives. (id: security-plus-4)

## CEH (track: ceh)
1. [Reconnaissance & Footprinting Techniques](https://owasp.org/www-project-web-security-testing-guide/latest/4-Web_Application_Security_Testing/01-Information_Gathering.html) — tutorial · 90 min  
    Hands-on guide to information gathering techniques used in ethical hacking and penetration testing. (id: ceh-1)
2. [Exploitation Fundamentals and Web Attacks](https://owasp.org/www-project-top-ten/) — tutorial · 120 min  
    Practical examples of common web exploitation techniques and defenses, aligned with CEH topics. (id: ceh-2)
3. [Metasploit for Penetration Testing](https://docs.rapid7.com/metasploit/) — tutorial · 90 min  
    Introduction to Metasploit framework for exploitation, post-exploitation, and proof-of-concept development. (id: ceh-3)
4. [Social Engineering Techniques and Defenses](https://www.sans.org/security-resources/social-engineering) — article · 60 min  
    Overview of social engineering attack vectors and practical countermeasures for ethical hackers. (id: ceh-4)

## CISSP (track: cissp)
1. [Security and Risk Management (NIST Risk Framework)](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-37/rev-2/final) — article · 120 min  
    Foundational risk management concepts and frameworks used in CISSP domains and enterprise programs. (id: cissp-1)
2. [Security Architecture and Engineering](https://www.iso.org/isoiec-27001-information-security.html) — article · 90 min  
    Standards and architecture principles for building secure systems and aligning to governance requirements. (id: cissp-2)
3. [Governance, Compliance & Legal Issues](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework) — tutorial · 75 min  
    NIST Cybersecurity Framework and governance practices relevant to compliance and risk reporting. (id: cissp-3)
4. [Security Operations and Incident Response](https://www.sans.org/white-papers/incident-response) — article · 75 min  
    Operational controls, monitoring, and structured incident response practices for enterprise security teams. (id: cissp-4)
